# The Dressing Room [MA1805 Spring Final Project]

##Game Theme

Our game is focused around the theme of ‘getting ready’ and the pressures faced, predominantly by women, who feel the need to look perfect at all times due to the expectations of our modern day society. Studies have found, largely among the teenage and young adult age group, that 80% of women feel that their looks are ‘the most important thing’ about them, clearly demonstrating that society puts unrealistic expectations on women https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/education-37543769 
. Our game was intended to be a commentary on this.

## Functionality

Using the standard controls of the ‘WASD’ keys, you can move the character around the tilemap. The aim of the game was to ‘collect’ all of the different makeup items by walking over them with your character, which symbolizes completing that step of your makeup routine. However , we significantly struggled with finding a working piece of code that could implement different makeup items being collected and thus, due to this only blusher compact icons can be collected, we have actually found this to benefit the game in such a way that it makes the aesthetics look tidier and allows the player to focus more on the script which is more important than what makeup is being collected.
As we are beginners at coding, we wanted to keep the main controls of the game simple but functional, so we chose to use these keys for navigation and the spacebar key for moving through the dialogue.

## Technical Design

We kept a stereotypically ‘girly’ design in mind when creating the different elements of our game. We also wanted to maintain a retro game style, so we used pixel art for all of our artistic elements as we felt this aligned with the traditionally retro aesthetic.
We chose pink as the main colour for the project, we found this was most fitting for the theme as we wanted to keep the look of the game light hearted whilst also appealing to a wide audience. We would have liked to add our own font into the start screen of the game, but struggled with this, though we used an image for the instructions page and therefore were able to have this customised to our style of art.
We have both made the art for the game ourselves, on Canva and on Procreate. Though this was very time consuming and challenging due to the complexity of pixel art, we are happy with this design choice as we feel it has contributed well to the aesthetics of the game overall and aligns with our theme successfully. Adding our own personal touch to this game was essential to us, as even though our code may not be the most complex, we feel that this personal element contributes to making it unique.

## How the Project Could be Extended

We are both aware that this game is not as complex as we could have achieved, however we both struggled with the time constraints and also ran into some technical issues with accessing the work that we had both individually written. We feel that if there was more time, we would have liked to add an element of time constraints to the game, so that each ‘level’ would get progressively harder and you would struggle more to complete it within the time. Not only would this contribute to our overall message of how women may struggle to meet societal expectations of how they should look when pressed for time, but it would also add to the overall user experience and would make the game more fun and challenging.
It would have also been nice to add the concept of choosing your own character, though we did not have time to research how to do this and add it into our game. Originally this was in our plan and we also created the art required for this, which is included below. Choosing your own character, though it may seem like a small addition, can be important for representation and would allow different users to feel more included.
